When a solved problem leaves no trace beyond terminal history, I have to hunt for the answer again next time. Writing down the reasoning gives my future self something to return to.

## Start with one clear record

There is no need to wait until I can write a complete tutorial. A clear problem, a useful troubleshooting process, and the conditions in which it applies are already worth keeping.

## Give each article a stable address

Notes can change; their links should stay. Give an article a stable address and mark its update date when adding new findings, so people can still follow earlier references to it.

## Leave room for what is still uncertain

State confirmed conclusions clearly, and be just as clear about what is not yet known. One article does not need to settle every question.

I will save this note today. The next time I need it, I hope it saves me a few detours.
